2 · Exposure and focus lock — supported, and honoured?

    
Each test samples the mean luminance of the left quarter of the frame for
      10 s. From second 3 to second 7, cover the right half of the frame with your hand (do not
      touch the left quarter). If exposure is really locked, the left quarter stays flat.

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

  


  

    
3 · Calibration through calibrate, and marker jitter

    
Plate with the four ArUco markers, no sand pattern needed. "Jitter" calibrates
      8 consecutive frames 1 s apart without touching anything: the spread is the noise floor the
      marker-position check must tolerate.

    

      
      
    

    

      
      
    

    
    

  


  

    
4 · One pattern frame through ingest.assess_frame

    
Form a pattern with sand (block 6 can play the tone), then grab. The label is a
      dummy (1 Hz) in the throwaway draft: this measures the gate and the timings, it labels nothing.
